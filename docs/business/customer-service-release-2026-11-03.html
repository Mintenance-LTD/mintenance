Customer terms, cancellation and support

Prepared 3 November 2026. The owner approved the customer terms, cancellation proposal and operating responsibilities on 2 November. This release keeps the public beta gate enabled. Publication is complete only after the reviewed commit, database migrations and mobile relay are deployed and verified.

Customer terms and agreements

The website and mobile app use the same approved terms revision, 2026-11-03. Customers can retain a copy. Signup records the declared revision; signing an agreement requires an explicit acceptance. Early-start requests and full-performance acknowledgements are separate, initially unchecked choices. Signing without requesting an early start remains possible.

Signatures are bound to the reviewed contract and quote. The database preserves the choices and exact wording and prevents signed proposals being silently rewritten. Acceptance by the required parties records the formation date and cancellation deadline. Agreement copies enter a durable email queue. A downloadable record is also available.

Older accepted agreements awaiting work require a customer review before work starts. That review provides a fresh cancellation-information period without fabricating a historical formation date or changing previous signatures. A customer may request early performance without being forced to acknowledge loss of the cancellation right after full performance.

Cancellation operations

A cancellation notice creates an urgent support case, retains its first receipt time and places held payments into administrative review. It prevents work starting while cancellation is unresolved. A release already claimed by the payment processor can still complete and needs investigation. A notice, acknowledgement or closed support case never invents a refund or releases a financial hold. Use the existing payment review and refund controls separately.

Responsibilities and limits

Gloire is the initial support lead and proposed owner for incident response, refunds/disputes and privacy requests, consistent with the approved operations checklist. Existing administrators are support operators; only the lead manages team permissions and beta access. Financial authority is not granted by support assignment.

Replies use noreply@mintenance.co.uk with admin@mintenance.co.uk as the reply-to inbox. In-app replies are attached to their case automatically. Incoming email replies still require monitoring in the mailbox and manual follow-up; this release does not claim automatic mailbox ingestion. Guest enquiries receive a reference and an operator email response. The guest form does not automatically email an unverified address.

This is a text support inbox, not a staffed live-chat or emergency service. Do not request passwords or complete card details. Use the existing secure job evidence and dispute tools for documents. Marketing campaigns require a separate consent-aware workflow. Physical Android acceptance and Apple Developer/TestFlight readiness remain separate launch checks.
