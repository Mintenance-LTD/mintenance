After the matching deployment

  1. Install the candidate; cold-start, background and reopen it. Confirm it reaches sign-in and the correct role dashboard without a splash-screen freeze.
  2. Sign in as homeowner, sign out, then sign in as contractor. Restart between accounts. No previous account's private information should remain visible.
  3. Complete the seven privacy and attachment checks, including unrelated-account denial and expired private image links.
  4. Search contractors by trade and city. Clear the search while loading: old results must not reappear. Open a contractor profile and return.
  5. Deny camera, photo and location permissions, then enable them through settings. Each screen should explain the missing permission and recover without crashing.
  6. Post a synthetic job with photos; submit and accept a bid; exchange assigned-job messages and confirm the other account receives them.
  7. Disable connectivity during a supported operation, restore it, then retry. Confirm one action is recorded and the screen does not falsely report success.
  8. Test notification consent, receipt while backgrounded, and opening the intended job/message. Confirm notification previews do not reveal another account's private data.
  9. Open supported app links and password recovery links; verify sign-out, recovery and account-switch behavior. Test a malformed percent-encoded link too: it should reject safely without freezing.
