Phase 2 phone checklist

Run on the release candidate after the matching web and database changes are deployed. Use two test accounts and synthetic photos/documents. The current installed build may not contain these fixes.

Record: mobile version/build, web release, date, homeowner test account and contractor test account. Do not send passwords.

  1. As a homeowner, browse contractors. Names, reviews and service area load. Another user's private phone, email and precise location should not appear in public discovery.
  2. In an assigned test job, send an image in both directions. The other participant should open it immediately. Close and reopen the conversation after its temporary link expires; history should produce a working new link.
  3. Sign into an unrelated account. The private job conversation and attachments must be inaccessible.
  4. Upload a contractor document, open it and delete it. A different contractor must not see or delete it.
  5. Open a saved assessment and a walkthrough assessment. Photos should match their findings. Repeat from an unrelated account: private assessments must not open.
  6. Delete a synthetic photo through its normal supported owner flow, then revisit history. A missing photo must not display another photo in its place.
  7. Public search should return contractor directory results, with city-level locations. Use the signed-in Jobs screen for job discovery.

Report pass/fail for each item, build number and any error shown. Keep real customer details out of screenshots.
