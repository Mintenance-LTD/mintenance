Web & mobile acceptance checklist

Draft — use after the matching backend and APK are confirmed. Prepared 7 October 2026. PR 1371 remains under verification. This checklist is not evidence that its tests have passed.

Before testing

Record the APK number, web deployment, account role, test time (UK), job ID and screenshots. Run each applicable test on web and Android, with separate homeowner and contractor accounts. Mark Web / Mobile / Result in your notes.

Use the sandbox for new payment, refund, dispute and account-deletion tests. For the existing real Lawn Care and Blocked Drain jobs, inspect the records and balances without creating another charge.

1 · Accounts and presentation

  • Register, verify email, sign in, reset password and sign out. Check that invalid input gives a helpful message.
  • Reopen the app and confirm the correct role and session. Check biometric sign-in and its sign-out warning on Android.
  • Open Settings, Certifications, Reviews, Subscription and Payouts. Confirm mint headers, readable buttons and consistent dialogs.
  • Open Terms and Privacy Policy from both profile and settings. Pages must load.
  • Edit the profile and biography; reopen on the other platform. Check public information is correct and private contact details are not exposed.
  • Navigate web using keyboard only, including forms, dialogs and main content. Check mobile with larger text.

2 · Complete a sandbox job

  1. Homeowner posts a job with photos, category, location, budget and dates. Reopen it on both platforms.
  2. Contractor finds the job and submits a bid. Homeowner accepts once; other bids stay consistent.
  3. Both parties open and sign the contract. Amounts, dates, parties and signature status agree.
  4. Exchange messages and verify delivery, unread counts and notifications after reopening.
  5. Fund the job with a sandbox card. Verify one payment, held escrow and the correct receipt. Test declined card and authentication challenge separately.
  6. Contractor uploads before and after photos and marks work complete. Homeowner opens Review Work; all authorised photos load.
  7. Homeowner approves the inspected work. Both platforms display the same review deadline and any genuine release blockers.
  8. Test an automatic-approval job separately. It must retain its trust, photo and dispute checks.
  9. Leave one review in each direction. Ratings and counts update without duplicates.

3 · Release and payout accuracy

  • Existing Lawn Care: expect completed escrow, £1.00 gross, £0.50 recorded fee and £0.50 earnings. Release occurred 7 October at 16:00 BST.
  • At the verified snapshot, contractor pending earnings total £1.20 (Drain £0.70 + Lawn £0.50), lifetime transferred £0.00. Later legitimate transfers will change these totals.
  • Finance, Reports, Escrow and Payouts must distinguish released earnings, pending transfer, Stripe transfer and bank arrival.
  • Confirm no £50 platform minimum remains. A positive eligible balance starts at £0.01; provider settlement and bank payout timing remain separate.
  • Open the contractor Stripe dashboard and confirm the correct live account. Record transfer reference and bank payout status when available.
  • In sandbox, retry a release and simulate a lost response. Confirm only one earnings credit. Engineer verification must confirm payout retries use one durable operation and never duplicate a Stripe transfer.
  • In sandbox, test dispute, refund, administrative hold and an unpaid-balance account deletion attempt. Each must preserve the appropriate funds and records.

4 · Recovery and cross-platform checks

  • Lose connectivity during photo upload, job submission and payment-status retrieval; reconnect and retry. Confirm no duplicate job, image or charge.
  • Switch between web and mobile after each key action. Status, amounts and available next actions must agree.
  • Check empty, loading and error states. A payout-status error must not incorrectly claim that a card was or was not charged.
  • Check expense categories and date filters; prior-month expenses must not inflate this month's total.
  • Test notification links opening the correct job or conversation; test permissions denied and later enabled.

Evidence to send back

For each failure: checklist step, Web or APK version, role, job ID, exact UK time, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot. Do not send passwords, card numbers or secret keys.
